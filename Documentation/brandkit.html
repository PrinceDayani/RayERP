Accessibility & Contrast

Brand burgundy is dark enough to clear WCAG AA (and AAA) against white in both directions — safe for buttons and links. The lighter hover tint and muted text clear AA only; don't use them for small critical text.

White on Brand8.7 : 1 · AAA
Brand on White8.7 : 1 · AAA
Hover on White5.1 : 1 · AA
Muted on White4.7 : 1 · AA

Do

  • Pair the 2px ring-primary focus ring with a 2px offset (globals.css)
  • Keep body text on --foreground (10% / 98%)
  • Use the burgundy for interactive affordances

Don't

  • Set small text in --brand-hover or status tints
  • Rely on color alone — pair status chips with a label
  • Drop focus outlines for aesthetics

Accessibility Checklist

The bar every screen should clear before ship.

Text meets WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large).
Every interactive element has a visible focus ring (2px, offset).
Status is never conveyed by color alone — pair with text or icon.
All inputs have associated labels; placeholders aren't the only label.
Icons that convey meaning have aria-label or adjacent text.
Tab order is logical; modals trap and restore focus.
Hit targets are at least 40×40px on touch.
Motion respects prefers-reduced-motion.
Tables use proper headers; data has a text alternative to charts.
Content reflows to 320px with no loss of function.
